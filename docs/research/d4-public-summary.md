# What kind of printable frame deserves further investigation?

D4 compared printable space frames, reinforced printed trusses, printed nodes with
rods or tubes, threaded reinforcement, replaceable metal bracing and a conventional
2020 extrusion reference. **No frame architecture has been selected or validated.**

The most promising next comparison is a deep printed truss with continuous metal
chords versus a tube-and-node structure with permanent, replaceable bracing. Both
could put material where it resists bending effectively while preserving printable
modules. Small solid rods remain useful axial-member candidates, but are relatively
flexible as long unsupported beams in the examples studied.

The connections may matter more than the metal. One hypothetical tube arm bends about
0.112 mm, while its assumed root joint adds another 0.180 mm. That is an illustrative
calculation, not a measurement or acceptable limit. Likewise, linking two beds can
reduce unequal sag but turn a small alignment error into large internal forces.

All eight bed arrangements retain separate support/constraint records. A common rigid
platform still cannot carry beds through unrelated printing heights in paired or mixed
modes. A stiff frame also cannot fix hot-sheet bow, docking capture problems or the
head collisions left unresolved by D1–D3.

Imperial and metric stock need distinct, reproducible interfaces. We found specific
catalog candidates and recorded missing tolerances and drawings rather than rounding
one stock standard into another. Hand-removable enclosure panels must leave permanent
bracing, load restraints and electrical protection in place.

The package contains [comparisons](d4-engineering-study.md), [editable diagrams](d4-diagrams.md),
[reproducible calculations](d4-calculations.md), [sources](d4-references.md) and
[verification limits](d4-verification.md). Next, investigate joint stiffness, compatible
bed-pair supports and complete motion/service envelopes. Detailed CAD needs further
review and authorization. The first physical proof remains **two-bed docking and
cross-seam printing**, with four individual sheets retained in the eventual machine.
