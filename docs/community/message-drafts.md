## GitHub Discussion: welcome (proposed pin)

```markdown
# Welcome to Project Tesseract — help investigate a four-bed RepRap

Project Tesseract explores a printer that could automatically move four nominal 180 × 180 mm heated beds horizontally between separate, paired, mixed and fully joined layouts, retaining four individual sheets at a common reconfiguration plane.

We are at early feasibility: no CAD, mechanism, firmware or prototype has been validated. The Phase 1 trade study is a proposal for critique, and the first physical proof remains two-bed docking and cross-seam printing. Nominal dimensions do not establish usable print area; four heads cooperating on one object is an unproven stretch goal.

The printable-first RepRap goal includes Ender 3-sized printed parts, separately reproducible imperial/metric reinforcement variants and a later 2020-extrusion edition. We intend to become fully open source, but licensing is undecided and no open-source permission grant is currently selected.

Start with https://github.com/VoidPhoenixApps/project-tesseract and its CONTRIBUTING.md. Introduce the question you want to explore, check current issues and PRs, and propose one small contribution. Source checks, diagrams, calculations, mechanical criticism and test plans all help. Challenge assumptions respectfully and show what evidence could change your mind.

GitHub keeps the durable decisions and evidence. Failed and inconclusive results belong in the record too. Research and documentation do not authorize physical tests; those require reviewed hazards, procedures, limits and appropriate supervision.

What small uncertainty would you like to help resolve?
```

## GitHub Discussion: first engineering challenge (proposed pin)

```markdown
# First challenge: can two heated beds repeatedly form a printable seam?

Before scaling to four moving beds, we need evidence for repeatable docking and printing across two retained individual sheets. No mechanism or gate has been validated.

Read https://github.com/VoidPhoenixApps/project-tesseract/blob/main/docs/phase1/README.md and coordinate with current issues/PRs before starting a parallel study. Its rankings and numerical criteria remain proposals for review.

Pick one assumption: carrier registration, hot sheet shape, seam gap/step, thermal expansion, lock detection or repeated-cycle drift. Cite the exact source revision and explain the failure you anticipate, the evidence supporting it and the observation that would distinguish alternatives. How would measurement uncertainty affect the conclusion?

Separate accurate carrier docking from actual hot surface behavior. Fixed segmented beds and attractive CAD are not proof of repeatably docking heated plates. Keep the required horizontal, fixed-height reconfiguration and four retained sheets in view; request explicit review if a requirement appears infeasible.

Please propose bounded research or a reviewed test plan here. Do not energize or move hardware on the strength of this discussion. Safe procedures, measurable acceptance limits, uncertainty, abort criteria and appropriate supervision must be reviewed first. Preserve negative results and link conclusions back to a GitHub issue or PR.
```
